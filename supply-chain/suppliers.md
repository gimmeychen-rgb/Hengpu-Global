# Suppliers System - Hengpu Global

## Purpose
This document defines the structured supplier network for Hengpu Global.

## Supplier Categories

### 1. Industrial Hardware Suppliers
- Hinges
- Fasteners
- Metal components
- Machinery parts

### 2. Mining Equipment Suppliers
- Exploration tools
- Drilling machines
- Transport equipment
- Safety systems

### 3. Raw Material Suppliers
- Metals
- Industrial minerals
- Construction materials

## Supplier Evaluation Criteria

- Production capacity
- Price stability
- Export experience
- Quality consistency
- Communication reliability

## Supplier Status Levels

- Tier 1: Strategic long-term partners
- Tier 2: Stable suppliers
- Tier 3: Trial suppliers

## Goal
Build a verified, scalable, and globally distributed supplier network.
