# Pricing Model - Hengpu Global

## Purpose
This document defines the pricing logic system for Hengpu Global supply chain operations.

---

## 1. Pricing Structure Framework

Final Price = Base Cost + Logistics + Risk Adjustment + Margin

---

## 2. Cost Components

### Base Cost
- Factory price
- Raw material cost
- Production cost

### Logistics Cost
- Domestic transport
- International shipping
- Customs clearance
- Destination delivery

---

## 3. Risk Adjustment Factors

- Country risk level
- Payment reliability
- Supplier stability
- Political / trade risk

Risk Levels:
- Low risk: +2% ~ +5%
- Medium risk: +5% ~ +10%
- High risk: +10% ~ +20%

---

## 4. Margin Strategy

### Standard Margin Range
- Industrial hardware: 8% - 15%
- Mining equipment: 10% - 25%
- Special projects: 15% - 35%

---

## 5. Client Pricing Strategy

### Government / Strategic Clients
- Lower margin
- Long-term contracts
- Stability priority

### Commercial Clients
- Standard margin
- Flexible negotiation

### High-risk Clients
- Higher margin
- Prepayment preferred

---

## 6. Quotation Rules

- Always separate cost components
- Never provide blind total price without breakdown
- Adjust pricing based on relationship level
- Maintain negotiation buffer (5% - 15%)

---

## 7. Goal

Build a transparent, adjustable, and scalable pricing system that supports global trade operations.
