# Clients System - Hengpu Global

## Purpose
This document defines the client classification and cooperation structure for Hengpu Global.

---

## 1. Client Categories

### 1. Government & Institutional Clients
- Mining departments
- Trade agencies
- Infrastructure projects
- Policy-driven partnerships

### Characteristics:
- Long-term cooperation
- High trust requirement
- Slow decision cycle
- Large project scale

---

### 2. Industrial Clients (Factories / Manufacturers)
- Hardware factories
- Machinery manufacturers
- Component suppliers

### Characteristics:
- Price-sensitive
- Stable repeat orders
- Fast negotiation cycle

---

### 3. Trading Partners / Intermediaries
- Local agents
- Import/export traders
- Regional distributors

### Characteristics:
- Flexible cooperation
- Fast deal cycles
- Margin sharing models

---

## 2. Trust Level System

### Tier 1: Strategic Partners
- Long-term contracts
- Exclusive cooperation potential

### Tier 2: Verified Partners
- Stable cooperation
- Medium trust level

### Tier 3: Trial Partners
- First-time cooperation
- High monitoring required

---

## 3. Client Evaluation Criteria

- Payment reliability
- Communication efficiency
- Order consistency
- Risk level (country + industry)
- Long-term potential

---

## 4. Cooperation Strategy

- Government: trust building first, profit second
- Industrial: efficiency and pricing optimization
- Traders: speed and flexibility

---

## 5. Goal

Build a structured global client network that supports scalable and repeatable trade operations.
