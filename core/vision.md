# Hengpu Vision

Hengpu is a global supply chain and AI-driven trading system focused on connecting manufacturers, miners, and global partners through structured digital infrastructure.

## Mission
To build a transparent, intelligent, and scalable global trade and resource collaboration network.

## Core Direction
- Global supply chain integration
- Mining project collaboration
- AI-powered decision making
- Digital automation of trade workflows

## Principle
Trust, structure, and long-term value creation.
